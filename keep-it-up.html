Keep It Up: Cargo Bay

You're responsible for a deeply questionable object in a low-poly cargo bay.

  • Click or tap the red balloon to send it upward. Each successful hit scores one point.
  • The balloon can drift sideways and bounce off the walls. It falls faster as your count increases.
  • This is first-person: WASD moves in the direction you face. Hold and drag on empty scenery, or swipe, to look around.
  • Your glowing floor ring shows how close the balloon must be before you can hit it.
  • Click or tap the actual balloon to hit it while it is within your ring. Space and the arrow keys do not hit.
  • Touching the floor ends the shift. You can start again immediately.
  • Pause at any time; your best score is stored in this browser.

The cargo-bay challenge is entirely separate from Crilo Daily, its badge collection, and leaderboards.
