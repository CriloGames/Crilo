Keep It Up: Cargo Bay

Keep one balloon in the air while ascending an open-air obstacle course made of floating mountain platforms.

  • Aim your crosshair at the balloon (or tap its visible body on touchscreens) and click to hit it while it's inside your glowing range ring. Clicking empty space won't hit.
  • The balloon travels away from your hit. Striking its left or right side steers it in the opposite direction; lower hits give more upward lift. There are no enclosing walls to bounce it off: an off-course balloon is lost.
  • This is third-person: the camera follows the visible character from behind. After your first balloon hit, WASD moves in the direction you face. Move the mouse to look around; Escape releases the cursor and pauses. Touch players can swipe.
  • Your glowing floor ring shows how close the balloon must be before you can hit it.
  • Only clicking the visible balloon body counts. Until your first successful hit, you cannot move or jump, and the balloon remains suspended. Your first hit unlocks movement and starts the fall timer. Space jumps but never hits the balloon.
  • Every stage climbs upward on walkable ramps and landings. Clear the raised hurdle bars with a higher Space jump, avoid the moving sweepers on tougher stages, and reach the summit gate after enough hits. Falling off the walkway or losing the balloon resets that stage.
  • Pause, select unlocked levels, or play fullscreen. Your unlocked courses and personal record stay saved in this browser.
