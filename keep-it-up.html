Keep It Up!

Tap or click directly on the balloon to send it upward. Each successful tap earns one bounce.

  • Don't let the balloon touch the floor.
  • Tap the left or right side to steer it as you bounce.
  • The balloon gets a little more lively as your score climbs.
  • Use Space or ↑ to bop it with the keyboard.
  • Pause any time. Your best bounce count stays on this browser.
