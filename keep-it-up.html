Keep It Up: Cargo Bay

You're responsible for a deeply questionable object in a low-poly cargo bay.

  • Click or tap inside the game to hit the balloon when it is inside your glowing range ring. It does not require pixel-perfect aiming.
  • The balloon can drift sideways and bounce off the walls. It falls faster as your count increases.
  • This is first-person: WASD moves in the direction you face. Move the mouse freely to look around. Escape releases the cursor and pauses. Touch players can swipe.
  • Your glowing floor ring shows how close the balloon must be before you can hit it.
  • Click or tap to hit while the balloon is in range. Space jumps over obstacles but never hits the balloon. Your first click starts the balloon physics so you can get your bearings.
  • Each level requires balloon hits plus reaching the exit. Avoid red floor strips, spinning beams and flashing tiles; if the balloon falls or an obstacle hits, retry that level.
  • Pause, select unlocked levels, or play fullscreen. Your unlocked courses and personal record stay saved in this browser.

The cargo-bay challenge is entirely separate from Crilo Daily, its badge collection, and leaderboards.
