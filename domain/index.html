HOW TO PLAY

How Much Was the Domain?

You get 5 domains. Move the slider to guess each domain's price, then lock it in.

1Read the domain and whether it is a documented sale or Crilo estimate.
2Move the logarithmic price slider from $1 to $250 million.
3Closer guesses earn more points, up to 1,000 each round.
4Your final score is out of 5,000. Your five best scores are saved on this device.

Crilo estimates are hypothetical domain-only game estimates, not professional appraisals.
