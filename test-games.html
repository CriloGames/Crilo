THE LAST LIGHT

Keep up with it.

Move your mouse into the light and click to start. Once it moves, leaving the light's edge ends your run.

No lives. No clicking targets. Just you and the light.

This experiment is local to your browser. It does not submit scores to Crilo.

How it works: The light gains momentum and gradually changes shape. The more your cursor presses against its edge, the more it pulls away. Following its center is steadier. Esc pauses; restart to try a different difficulty. A touch screen also works by dragging while holding.
