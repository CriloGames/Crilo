Full game rules
  • Press Start to reveal the daily word. You get two seconds to read it before guessing opens.
  • Keep every daily letter in order. For example, NET fits in NuggET. Each repeated letter needs its own spot.
  • Use single words in the game’s English dictionary. Capitalization is ignored. Spaces and punctuation are excluded.
  • Submit with Enter or the arrow. Each distinct, longer word earns one point. You can retry a missed guess immediately.
  • Everyone gets the same 3–5 letter word. Each has at least 25 answers, including 10 common ones.
  • You get one timed round per browser each day. Refreshing resumes the same clock.
