    How to climb

    Each pitch has six word holds and a 15-second clock. Your first scoring word locks in. Unlisted words can be retried until time expires. A timeout earns zero. Enter one word that pairs with as many as you can, in either order. For example, BOOK links NOTE and CLUB through notebook and book club.

    Scan the holds clockwise. Opposite pairs are 1 & 4, 2 & 5, and 3 & 6. An opposite pair adds 10 points. A full six-hold send scores 200 in total.
