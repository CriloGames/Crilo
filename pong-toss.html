How to Play Pong Toss

From a first-person office desk, throw your selected object through the opening of a cup, mug, bin or basket. Each stage uses its own target position, furniture and obstacle arrangement. The important shot mechanics are highlighted so you can read the challenge quickly.

  • Real rebounds: The ball can ricochet from desks, shelves, cabinets, books, computers, walls, floors, moving obstacles, hoop rims and cup rims. Metal rebounds differently from wood and softer objects. The ball loses energy when it bounces, and special pads remain more powerful.
  • Mouse and touch: Hold the mouse button or touch the game to begin charging. Drag while holding to move your aim without interrupting power. Release to throw. The dotted arc updates as you steer.
  • Keyboard: Use Arrow keys or WASD to adjust aim while holding Space to charge; release Space to throw. Left/right controls have a wider and more sensitive range. You can also move the mouse while holding Space. R restarts the stage and Esc pauses.
  • Early tricks: Learn aiming, fly through the first hoop (level 4), and experiment with rebounds, moving cups, air jets and shutters.
  • Wall ricochets: Touch the striped wall cushion to reflect the ball. If a level has two numbered wall banks, hit them in order before scoring.
  • Hoop challenges: Pass through each numbered ring in order before sinking the ball. Later hoops move or appear in pairs.
  • Directional fans: Local air jets push the ball sideways or upward only while it travels through the visible airflow. Crosswinds and gusts can also affect the shot.
  • Advanced combinations: Later levels feature spring carts at varied distances, including numbered two-pad sequences. Hit required pads in order, time hinged lids, and combine rebounds with fans, moving barriers and hoops. Pads never aim the ball at the cup.
  • Complete all 50 levels with the ping-pong ball to unlock the frisbee, then mini basketball, heavy ball, and featherlight ball in order. Every object has different physics and its own saved progress. Lit symbols show which objects have cleared a level.
