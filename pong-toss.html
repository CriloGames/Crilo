How to Play Pong Toss

From a first-person office desk, flick the small orange practice ball through the opening of a cup, mug, bin or basket. Each stage has different target placements, furniture and obstacles.

  • Mouse and touch: Drag upward and release to flick, as in classic touchscreen throwing games. Swipe direction controls the angle and strength. Alternatively, move your mouse to aim, hold to charge, and release to shoot. Follow the dotted arc.
  • Keyboard: Use Arrow keys or WASD to adjust your shot. Hold Space to charge, then release Space to throw. R restarts the stage and Esc pauses.
  • Levels 1–10: Precision throws through real foreground clutter. For bank shots, the rubber tray changes the ball's speed and bounce height, not its destination.
  • Levels 11–20: Targets slide, move nearer or farther, rise and fall, orbit, or follow figure-eight routes. Each room uses different furniture.
  • Levels 21–30: Oscillating desk fans and crosswinds bend your throw.
  • Levels 31–40: Hand-placed filing cabinets, monitors and swinging drawer barriers interrupt different shot paths.
  • Levels 41–50: Spring pads bounce the ball upward, but never aim it for you. Control the throw angle, direction and power to land the rebound yourself.
  • You may retry as often as you like. Finish in two throws or fewer for 3 stars, three or four throws for 2 stars, or five or more for 1 star.

All Pong Toss progress is stored locally on this device. It does not affect Crilo Daily or leaderboards.
