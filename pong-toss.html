How to Play Pong Toss

Your goal is to land a ping-pong ball inside the opening of the target cup, bowl, bucket or basket.

  • Mouse and touch: Move or drag inside the 3D game to aim. Press and hold to build power; release to throw. Follow the dotted flight preview.
  • Keyboard: Use Arrow keys or WASD to adjust your shot. Hold Space to charge, then release Space to throw. R restarts the stage and Esc pauses.
  • Levels 1–10: Classic distance shots and static obstacles.
  • Levels 11–20: Moving containers — anticipate where they'll be when the ball arrives.
  • Levels 21–30: Crosswinds curve your shot.
  • Levels 31–40: Moving barriers block your flight path.
  • Levels 41–50: Glowing bounce pads launch the ball upward.
  • You may retry as often as you like. Finish in two throws or fewer for 3 stars, three or four throws for 2 stars, or five or more for 1 star.

All Pong Toss progress is stored locally on this device. It does not affect Crilo Daily or leaderboards.
