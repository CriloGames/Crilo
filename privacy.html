YOUR DATA, YOUR BUSINESS

PRIVACY, PLEASE.

What Crilo collects, what stays private, and what could change in the future.

Last updated October 11, 2026 · Privacy Policy

01Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Crilo (“we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information when you visit crilo.fun, play our games, create an account, or contact us. Crilo is currently a free browser-game website with optional tips.

Current approach: We do not currently sell personal information or use third-party advertising networks for behavioral advertising. We may consider advertising or other monetization later, but this statement describes what we do now—not a grant of permission for future uses.

02Information we collect

Account details: If you sign in, we receive your email address for magic-link authentication, an account ID, your chosen username, name color, preferences, and account-related security information.

Gameplay and social activity: We store saved official Daily runs, scores, spin results, badges, achievements, leaderboards, drawings, and any friend requests or connections you choose to use. Owner-only Test Runs are kept separately from competitive records.

Support and moderation: If you report an issue, we receive the description and details you provide. Our report system also processes the page address, browser information, and a privacy-preserving hash derived from the network address to limit spam. Drawings and other public content can be reviewed or flagged for rule enforcement, including automated checks and manual review.

Technical information: Hosting, authentication, and other service providers may process IP addresses, device/browser details, timestamps, security logs, and request metadata as needed to deliver and protect the site.

03Browser storage and site activity

Crilo uses your browser’s local storage to keep preferences, login/session information, guest-game progress, temporary drawing recovery data, and daily activity markers. You can remove local browser data using your browser settings, but this may sign you out or erase unsaved guest progress.

We collect approximate daily site-activity totals—such as visitors, guest starts, guest completions, and result-sharing actions—through a small counter. These event totals do not submit a persistent visitor ID, email address, drawing, or account profile. Browser-side markers help avoid repeated counts. Network and hosting providers still receive ordinary request information.

04How we use information

  • To sign you in, preserve your account, and provide game, leaderboard, badge, profile, and friends features.
  • To verify official Daily results, prevent cheating and duplicate attempts, and administer fair competition.
  • To display the content and profile details that you choose to make public through Crilo.
  • To address bug reports, enforce community rules, investigate abuse, and secure the service.
  • To understand approximate site activity, maintain performance, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

05What other people can see

Your username, selected name color, public profile information, leaderboard scores, achievements, and drawings associated with publicly displayed runs may be visible to other visitors. Do not draw, upload, or submit sensitive information that you do not want displayed publicly. Your sign-in email address is not intended to appear on public profiles or leaderboards.

06Service providers and disclosures

We use providers to operate specific features, including Supabase (authentication, database, and backend services), Resend (transactional emails), GitHub Pages (website hosting), and third-party software delivery networks used by the site. These providers process information in connection with delivering their services under their own applicable terms and privacy practices.

Optional payments are handled by PayPal, not by a Crilo-operated credit-card form. PayPal handles payment details under its own privacy policy; we may receive payment-related information that PayPal makes available to the recipient.

We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect users or the service, or as part of a business transfer, subject to applicable legal requirements.

07Advertising and potential future data uses

Right now: Crilo does not sell users’ personal information and does not currently integrate third-party advertising networks for personalized ads.

In the future: Crilo might introduce display ads, sponsorships, personalized advertising, aggregate data products, or other partnerships. Depending on how they work, some arrangements could legally qualify as a “sale” or “sharing” of personal information, even without money changing hands. We will describe material new practices before putting them into effect and provide legally required notices, choices, opt-outs, and consent where applicable. We will not rely on a future-policy statement alone to justify retroactive uses of information collected under earlier promises.

If we introduce a practice subject to applicable sale/sharing opt-out laws, we will provide the required opt-out method and honor applicable opt-out preference signals, including Global Privacy Control where legally required, before beginning that practice.

08Your choices and privacy requests

You can change supported account preferences in Settings. Signed-in players can request immediate account deletion using the two-step Delete Account control in Settings. Deletion removes associated account and game records from active systems as implemented; limited service-provider records, security logs, backups, or information we must retain under law may persist for a period.

Depending on where you live and which privacy laws apply, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, obtain a copy of, or limit particular uses of personal information. Email crilogame@gmail.com with “Privacy Request” in the subject. We may need to verify that the requester owns the account before disclosing or changing account data.

09How long data is kept

We generally retain account and game records while your account remains active or as needed for operation, moderation, integrity, and lawful purposes. Guest-game information stored only in your browser may remain until you clear it or the game removes it. Support requests, security information, and provider backups can have different retention periods. We do not promise that every copy disappears instantly after a deletion request.

10Security and children

We use authentication, access controls, and other safeguards appropriate to the service, but no online system is guaranteed to be completely secure.

Crilo is intended for a general audience and is not intended for children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has supplied personal information to us, contact crilogame@gmail.com so we can review the report and take appropriate action.

11Changes to this policy

We may revise this Privacy Policy as the site changes. We will update the date above and, when legally required, provide additional notice or obtain consent before a material change takes effect. Changes do not automatically override rights or promises governing information collected previously.

12Contact

Crilo is the operator of this website. For privacy questions, requests, or concerns, please email crilogame@gmail.com. You can also return to Crilo’s homepage.
