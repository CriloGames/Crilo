What are badges?

Badges are achievements you earn by playing official Daily runs, hitting special wheel combinations, building streaks, and exploring Crilo.

Bright tiles are earned. Light tiles are still locked. Each set goes from easier to harder badges, and its progress bar shows how many you have. Tap a badge to read its full unlock requirement.
